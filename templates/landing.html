Frequently Asked Questions

Simple answers to common questions about your new video hub.

How do the videos get saved?
Our background helper downloads the raw video MP4 files directly to your machine's media storage folder, registering them in your local app database so they can play instantly without an active internet connection.
Is there a subscription fee or limit?
No subscription fees or usage caps. Because you run and host the app locally on your own computer, you have absolute ownership over your storage capacity and network consumption.
How hard is it to set up?
It takes just a few minutes. We've written one-click automated setup scripts that install the browser handler (Chrome) and start the backend service. Once set up, it runs quietly in the background without needing your attention.
